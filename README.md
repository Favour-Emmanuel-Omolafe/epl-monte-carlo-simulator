# ⚽ Premier League 15-Year Monte Carlo Simulation Engine (2026–2041)

An interactive sports analytics engine that models long-term Premier League title races, European qualification, and squad turnover using Dixon-Coles Poisson goal expectations, bench depth penalties, and regulatory financial constraints (PSR/SCR).

🔗 **Live Interactive App:** *Live deployment running on Streamlit Community Cloud (Link updating)*

---

## 📌 Executive Summary & Problem Formulation
Predicting a football league deterministically over a 15-year horizon is fundamentally flawed due to managerial shifts, squad ageing curves, and unexpected regulatory shocks. 

Rather than treating football outcomes as a rigid, static formula, this project models the Premier League as a **stochastic system**. It runs hundreds of 15-year parallel seasons via the **Monte Carlo method**, generating robust probability distributions for title contenders, European berths, and relegation risks from 2026 to 2041.

---

## ⚙️ Analytical Architecture & Core Mechanics

### 1. Match Outcome Engine (Adjusted Poisson)
Match-level scoring expectations are modeled using normalized attack strength ($\alpha$) and defense weakness ($\beta$) scaled by league-wide goal averages:
$$\lambda_{\text{home}} = \alpha_{\text{home}} \times \beta_{\text{away}} \times \bar{G}_{\text{home}}$$
$$\mu_{\text{away}} = \alpha_{\text{away}} \times \beta_{\text{home}} \times \bar{G}_{\text{away}}$$
Scorelines are generated by drawing random discrete events from Poisson distributions parameterized by $\lambda$ and $\mu$.

### 2. Real-World Volatility & Shocks
- **Squad Fragility & Depth:** Teams experience random injury shocks. Elite squads with high bench depth scores absorb injuries with minimal degradation (~3%), while thin rosters suffer sharp performance penalties (~15–20%).
- **Disciplinary Volatility:** Accounts for yellow/red card frequencies, penalizing high-card rosters during tactical transitions.
- **Asymmetric Matchups ("Giant Killers"):** Distinct tactical profiles (e.g., Brentford, Brighton) receive situational efficiency multipliers when facing possession-heavy top-tier sides.

### 3. Multi-Season Economic & Regulatory Cycles
- **European Reinvestment:** Top 4 finishers receive compound rating gains reflecting Champions League prize distributions and commercial windfalls.
- **Financial Fair Play (PSR/SCR):** At-risk clubs face stochastic regulatory reviews. Triggering an SCR breach applies mandatory 6-to-8 point deductions and forced squad asset sales (rating degradation).
